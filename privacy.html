Privacy Policy

Short version: this site does not track you. No analytics, no ads, no accounts, no third-party scripts.

Cookies we use

Only three, all created by this site and all optional: branduzzo-lang remembers your language, branduzzo-todo remembers what you typed in the notepad, branduzzo-cursor remembers the cursor you picked in the secret menu. Nothing is sent anywhere: they live in your browser and you can delete them at any time from your browser settings.

What we do not use

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no third-party embeds, no fonts from other servers. Your IP is visible to the hosting provider like on any website, and that is all. If you decline the banner, no cookie is written and your language choice stays in localStorage.

Questions about privacy? Contact me at hello@branduzzo.it.
